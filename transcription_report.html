Metrics & Training Context

Omni Phase1 (Base) Training: The foundational model was trained utilizing a combination of the LibriSpeech, VCTK, and Common Voice datasets to establish baseline acoustic understanding.

What is WER? Word Error Rate is the standard metric for speech recognition accuracy. It is calculated as: WER = (Substitutions + Deletions + Insertions) / Total Words. A lower WER indicates better performance.

What is Normalized WER? Raw WER penalizes non-acoustic differences like casing ("Apple" vs "apple") or punctuation. Normalized WER applies a text standardization pipeline to both the reference and output text before calculation, providing a much truer measure of actual listening accuracy.
